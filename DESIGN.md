## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, health-focused nutrition brand for modern pet owners. The emotional tone balances clinical nutritional authority with radiant affection and joyful canine vitality. The visual identity avoids juvenile cartoon tropes in favor of an elevated, premium, and human-grade aesthetic.

The interface style merges modern tactile warmth with clean minimalism:
- **Warm, Sunlit Tactility:** Surfaces rely on soft cream layers rather than sterile cold grays, evoking whole-food ingredients and domestic comfort.
- **Dynamic Vitality:** Vibrant coral-red anchors call-to-actions and health markers, providing energetic contrast against warm golden ambers.
- **Spacious Editorial Discipline:** Ample negative space prevents visual clutter, allowing nutritional facts, ingredient transparency, and subscription controls to feel effortless and deliberate.
- **Friendly Geometry:** Hyper-rounded containers and fluid pill badges cultivate an approachable, protective, and welcoming atmosphere.

## Colors

The palette captures active canine happiness and whole-food warmth while retaining high contrast and legibility:

- **Primary (`#F59E0B` - Radiant Amber):** Represents wholesome vitality, natural grains, and sunlit energy. Used for primary branding elements, active selection states, high-tier highlight cards, and focal interactive accents.
- **Secondary (`#EF4444` - Vibrant Coral Red):** Conveys love, heartbeat vitality, and urgent conversion power. Reserved for primary conversion buttons (e.g., "Personalize Your Plan", "Subscribe & Save"), crucial nutritional badges, and celebratory states.
- **Tertiary (`#FEF3C7` - Soft Golden Cream):** Acts as a comforting container surface, pill background, and hover tint for interactive cards.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` - Deep Warm Slate):** Replaces harsh pure black to anchor text hierarchy, iconography, and high-contrast structural dividers with deep, readable warmth.
- **Canvas & Surface System:** 
  - Base canvas: `#FFFDF9` (Ivory Cream).
  - Raised cards & containers: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White) and `#FAF7F2` (Warm Oat).
  - Border strokes: `#F1EADF` (Soft Bone).

## Typography

The type system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to project contemporary, rounded warmth without losing crisp geometric precision.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold and extrabold weights with negative letter-tracking. This creates punchy, confident titles that evoke modern consumer packaged goods rather than clinical pharmaceuticals.
- **Body:** Set with generous line heights to ensure nutritional ingredients, portion breakdowns, and FAQ content remain legible and relaxed.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Utilize uppercase or semi-bold tracking for badges, puppy-to-senior stage pills, and calorie counters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a standard 12-column fluid grid on desktop transitioning into 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile, backed by an 8pt spatial baseline:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters and max content boundary of `1280px` centered within 3rem margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters and 2rem outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 1rem (`16px`) gutters and 1.25rem (`20px`) outer page margins.
- **Breathing Room:** Content blocks (e.g., custom meal composition, testimonials, and pet profiling steps) utilize `space-xl` internally to maintain an uncluttered, high-end feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient drop shadows mixed with subtle micro-borders, avoiding harsh dark cast shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Surface flush with canvas (`#FFFDF9`), framed with a 1px border of `#F1EADF`. Used for secondary fieldsets and inactive cards.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Badges):** Layered over background with `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`. Imparts a gentle golden glow.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Floating Elements):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06)`. Elevates active product tiers, sticky bottom bars, and selection cards.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.16)`. Combined with a softened backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The shape system embraces hyper-rounded, organic curves reflecting comfort, tail-wagging energy, and safety:

- **Base Radius (`roundedness: 3`):** Default UI controls (inputs, smaller containers) inherit a 1rem (`16px`) corner radius.
- **Cards and Large Panels (`rounded-2xl`):** Feature 1.5rem (`24px`) to 2rem (`32px`) radius, creating soft, pet-friendly silhouettes.
- **Pill Elements (`rounded-full`):** All actionable buttons, status chips, nutrient badges, and avatar frames use full continuous circular curvature (9999px).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight `700`, shape `rounded-full`, horizontal padding `2rem`, vertical padding `0.875rem`. Resting state has a warm coral-tinted shadow; hover state scales up to `1.02` with an ambient glow (`#DC2626`).
- **Secondary Action:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#F59E0B`, font-weight `700`, shape `rounded-full`. Hover switches to solid `#F59E0B` with white text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#1E293B`, subtle hover background of `#FAF7F2`.

### Chips & Pet Badges
- **Nutrient & Age Badges:** Fully rounded pill shapes. Soft amber background (`#FEF3C7`) with rich amber text (`#B45309`) and small icon prefixes (e.g., grain-free, omega-3, puppy formula).
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Border 1.5px solid `#F1EADF`, background `#FFFFFF`. Selected state transitions to solid `#F59E0B` with `#FFFFFF` text and no outline.

### Cards
- **Product & Meal Cards:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` background, `24px` radius, bordered with `1px solid #FAF7F2`. Includes a generous 1.5rem internal padding and elevation Level 1.
- **Active Subscription Card:** Outlined with a 2px `#F59E0B` stroke and subtle warm amber gradient halo.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Height 52px, `rounded-2xl`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #F1EADF`, typography `body-md`. Focus state uses `border-color: #F59E0B` and an outer ring glow of `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`.
- **Radio & Checkbox Controls:** Circular container with `24px` dimension. Checked state renders a vibrant `#EF4444` background with a crisp white inner icon or dot.

### Specialized Pet Profile Selector
- **Dog Profile Avatar Card:** Segmented selector cards with rounded avatar slots, pet weight toggles, and breed badges, featuring an interactive lift animation (`translate-y: -2px`) on hover.
